Privacy Policy for Memo Keeper

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

Summary: Memo Keeper respects your privacy. We don't collect, store, or share any of your personal data. All your notes and data stay completely private on your device.

Introduction

UK Solutions ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Memo Keeper mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information.

Memo Keeper is a simple notes application that stores all data locally on your device using Hive database. We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Access your notes or their content
  • Transmit any data to our servers or any third party
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • Require account registration or login
  • Request unnecessary permissions
  • Sync your data to cloud services

Data Storage

Completely Offline:

  • All notes are stored locally on your device using Hive (a secure local database)
  • Your notes never leave your device
  • No internet connection is required to use the app
  • If you uninstall the app, all your data is deleted from your device
  • We have no access to your notes or any data stored in the app

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you reminders for notes (optional, only if you set reminders)
  • Storage: To save notes locally on your device

These permissions are only used for the core functionality of the app and do not involve any data collection or transmission.

Third-Party Services

Memo Keeper does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks that may collect information used to identify you.

Security

We value your trust in using Memo Keeper. Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your device's security features protect your notes
  • We recommend using device lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face unlock)
  • We recommend regular device backups for data safety
  • No data is transmitted over the internet, eliminating online security risks

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the app works completely offline and doesn't collect any data, it is safe for users of all ages.

Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain links to other sites (such as this privacy policy page). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data:

  • There is no data to access, modify, or delete on our end
  • You have complete control over your notes on your device
  • You can delete notes, clear all data, or uninstall the app at any time
