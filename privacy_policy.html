Privacy Policy for Memo Keeper

Last Updated: June 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Memo Keeper is designed with privacy in mind. Your notes remain stored locally on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. However, the application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, and Google or its partners may collect certain device information for advertising and analytics purposes in accordance with their own privacy policies.

1. Introduction

UK Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Memo Keeper mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use Memo Keeper.

2. Your Notes and Personal Data

Your notes stay on your device.

  • All notes are stored locally using Hive database.
  • We do not upload your notes to our servers.
  • We cannot read your notes.
  • We cannot recover deleted notes.
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not require login.
  • We do not synchronize your notes to cloud storage.

3. Information We Collect

Memo Keeper itself does not collect or store your notes or personal content.

However, the application uses Google AdMob for displaying advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • Diagnostic information
  • Performance information
  • Ad interaction information
  • Device information

Such information is collected and processed by Google according to Google's own Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Memo Keeper displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may use cookies, mobile advertising identifiers, or similar technologies to:

  • Display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud
  • Improve advertising services
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized ads where applicable

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob

5. Local Data Storage

Memo Keeper stores all user-created notes locally on your own device.

  • No cloud backup is provided.
  • No remote synchronization is performed.
  • No note content is transmitted to UK Solutions.
  • You are responsible for backing up your own device.

6. Notifications

If you create reminders, Memo Keeper may request notification permission in order to deliver local reminder notifications.

Reminder notifications are generated locally on your own device and are not sent through our servers.

7. Permissions

Depending on your device and Android version, the app may request permissions such as:

  • Notification permission
  • Alarm scheduling permission
  • Boot completed permission (to restore reminders)
  • Internet access (required for advertisements)

These permissions are used only for application functionality and advertising services.

8. Children's Privacy

Memo Keeper is not specifically directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising providers may process limited technical information in accordance with their own policies.

9. Data Security

We value your privacy and security.

  • Your notes remain on your own device.
  • We recommend protecting your device using PIN, password, or biometrics.
  • We recommend maintaining regular device backups.

10. Third-Party Services

Memo Keeper currently uses:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies separately.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date on this page.

12. Your Rights

Since your notes remain stored only on your device:

  • You control your own notes.
  • You may edit or delete notes at any time.
  • Uninstalling the application removes locally stored data.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

  • UK Solutions
  • Email: apps.uksolutions@gmail.com
  • Bangalore, Karnataka, India
